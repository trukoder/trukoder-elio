our little story

A real bakery. Real ingredients. Real people.

We’re a small family-owned bakery that’s spent over a decade learning what it takes to make bread and pastries the right way — slowly, by hand, with the best ingredients we can find.

1
Up before sunrise, every day
Our bakers are in by 4am so everything in the case is made that day. What doesn’t sell goes to the food bank, never resold the next day.
2
Local flour, real butter, seasonal fruit
We work with farms and mills across Southern California. Stone-milled flour, European-style butter, fruit in season — because it tastes better.
3
Old techniques, no shortcuts
Sourdough that ferments 24 hours. Croissants laminated by hand. No commercial mixes, no dough conditioners, no preservatives. Ever.
4
Family owned, valley grown
We know our regulars by name and remember how they take their coffee. This is our neighborhood bakery — and you can taste it in every loaf.

a day at the bakery

The dough doesn’t sleep.

Bread is patient and we have to be too. Here’s a peek at what goes on between sundown and sunrise to get a fresh loaf in the case for you.

01
~→
Mix & rest

The night before, our starter wakes up. Flour, water, salt, time. The dough rests in the cold for twenty hours, building flavor.

02
~→
Shape by hand

Around 4am the bakers shape every loaf and pastry. No machines — just hands, a bench knife, and a lot of muscle memory.

03
~→
Into the oven

Steam, heat, the slow burn. Bread bakes for an hour. Croissants, twenty minutes. The smell drifts out before we even open.

04
Out on the counter

By 6:30am the case is full and the coffee is on. We unlock the door, you walk in, and the rest takes care of itself.
